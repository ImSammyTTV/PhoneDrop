Set up PhoneDrop

One time, so this device can use PhoneDrop securely

1. Install the PhoneDrop certificate

Download certificate

2. Trust it

iPhone / iPad: tap Allow, then open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap PhoneDrop Local CA and Install. Then go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and switch on PhoneDrop Local CA.

Android: open Settings, search for CA certificate, choose Install anyway and pick the downloaded ca.crt.

This certificate can only vouch for devices on your home network or tailnet, never real websites. Your browser needs it before it allows encryption and clipboard access.

3. Open PhoneDrop

Open PhoneDrop

Then tap Share → Add to Home Screen to open it in one tap next time, at home or away.
